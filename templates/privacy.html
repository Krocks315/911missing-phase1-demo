What We Collect

When you submit a report, tip, or sighting, we collect what you enter — names, descriptions, locations, dates, contact information if you provide it, and photos if you upload them. If you allow location access in your browser, we may collect your device's GPS coordinates at the time of submission.

How We Use It

Information submitted through this site is used to build and review missing-persons case records and to connect tips and sightings to the right case. We do not sell, rent, or use submitted information for advertising.

Who Sees It

Submissions are reviewed by authorized staff and volunteers of {{ org }}. In a production system, relevant information may also be shared with law enforcement in connection with an active case — that partnership is not yet in place, and this demo does not share any data outside our own systems.

Anonymous Submissions

Contact information is optional on tip and sighting forms. You can submit without identifying yourself.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information, though no system can guarantee complete security. Our data retention and security practices are still being finalized — see the Data Retention Note and Security & Data Protection page for current status.
