About Us

{{ org }} started from a simple observation: when someone goes missing, families are almost always left to organize the search themselves — printing flyers, posting on social media, calling everyone they know — while the clock keeps running. We're building a volunteer network designed to close that gap: a place where reports, tips, and sightings can move fast, get reviewed by a real person, and eventually connect directly to the people equipped to act on them.

We're early. What you see on this site today is a Phase 1 demo, built to prove the foundation works before we build the rest on top of it — and we'd rather show that honestly than dress it up as more than it is.
