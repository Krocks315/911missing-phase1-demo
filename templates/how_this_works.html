How This Demo Works

About a 2-minute read. Plain and honest, on purpose.

This site is a working prototype for {{ org }}'s Phase 1 build. It's not a finished product, and it's not live in production — but it's also not a mockup with fake buttons. Here's exactly what's real right now, and what still needs to be built.

What's Real

  • The forms actually work. Report Someone Missing, Submit a Tip, and Report a Sighting all submit live data — right now, into an isolated demo database, not a public one.
  • The database actually writes the records. When you submit a form, a real row gets created. Nothing here is a static screenshot pretending to be a working app.
  • The intake structure is real. The fields we ask for, the flow from report to case to tip to sighting — that's the actual structure we'd build on for a production system, not a rough sketch of one.
  • An eventual email notification flow is planned and designed for, so that a tip or sighting can reach the right person without anyone needing to sit and refresh a dashboard. That piece isn't wired up yet in this demo.

What's Not Built Yet

  • The case data is fictional. Every case on this site — names, ages, descriptions, circumstances — is invented for demonstration purposes. None of it describes a real person or a real case. Each sample case is clearly labeled as a sample.
  • There's no law enforcement integration. Nothing submitted through this demo reaches any police department, sheriff's office, or law enforcement database. That connection doesn't exist yet, and we won't claim otherwise anywhere on this site.
  • There's no live map yet. Location data is collected on the sighting form, but there's no public or internal map view built on top of it yet.
  • There's no real staff review dashboard yet. Submissions land in the database, but the tools a real coordinator would use to triage, verify, and act on them haven't been built. Right now, reviewing a submission means someone opening the database directly — see the demo admin review mockup for what that will eventually look like.

Why Show It This Way

We'd rather show the board something honest and early than something polished and fake. Everything marked "real" above is real. Everything marked "not built yet" is on the roadmap, not already done. That's the whole point of a Phase 1 demo — proving the foundation works before we build the rest on top of it.
