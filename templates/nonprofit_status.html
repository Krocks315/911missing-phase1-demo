How Nonprofit Status Works, in Plain Terms

A 501(c)(3) organization is recognized by the IRS as operating for a charitable, educational, or public-benefit purpose. That status is what allows donations to be tax-deductible for donors, and it comes with reporting requirements — annual filings, public disclosure of certain financial information, and restrictions on political activity. Organizations typically incorporate at the state level first, then apply to the IRS for federal tax-exempt recognition.

We'll update this page with our actual EIN, filing date, and determination letter status as soon as each is final. Until then, please treat any donation as a contribution to an organization still completing its nonprofit filing — not yet a confirmed tax-deductible gift.
