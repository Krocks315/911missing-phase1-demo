What we do

When a family reports someone missing, the clock matters more than almost anything else. {{ org }}'s job is to close the gap between "my loved one is gone" and "people are actively looking" — with a structured intake, a clear public status feed, and a way for tips and volunteers to reach the right case fast.

What we will never promise

We will not tell a family that law enforcement has been notified through this app unless that is literally true. Police departments run their own closed dispatch systems — no outside app has a legitimate door into that. What we can do, and do honestly: generate a law-enforcement-ready incident report, and help pre-fill a submission to NamUs, the Department of Justice's national missing-persons database.

Every case, reviewed by a human

Every report that comes through this intake gets a real person's eyes on it before anything is escalated. That's not red tape — it's what keeps a hoax report from triggering a real-world mobilization, and it's what makes this organization trustworthy to the agencies, donors, and volunteers this mission depends on.
