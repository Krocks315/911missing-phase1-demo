The Mission

When someone goes missing, the first hours matter more than almost anything else — and right now, most families are on their own for it: making flyers, posting on social media, hoping the right person sees it in time. {{ org }} exists to close that gap. We're building a volunteer network that activates the moment a case opens — so reporters, tipsters, and volunteers can get information moving immediately, with the goal of connecting that network to law enforcement once the partnerships and infrastructure are in place.

What you're looking at today is our Phase 1 demo: a working proof of concept showing the intake system, the case structure, and the reporting flow that everything else gets built on.

What We're Building Toward

  • A real, secure home for case data and tips — not a spreadsheet
  • Formal law enforcement partnerships so tips reach the right hands, not just our database
  • A staff/volunteer review dashboard so submissions get triaged fast
  • Public case pages that stay accurate and up to date
