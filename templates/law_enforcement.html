Law Enforcement Partnership

Honest status: no formal partnership with any law enforcement agency exists yet. This page describes the relationship we're working toward, not one that's active today.

Right now, nothing submitted through this site — a report, a tip, or a sighting — is automatically sent to any police department, sheriff's office, or law enforcement database. We will never tell a family that law enforcement has been notified through this app unless that is literally true.

What We're Building Toward

Our goal is a formal working relationship with law enforcement partners so that verified tips and sightings can reach the right investigator quickly, without slowing down or duplicating an agency's own process. In the meantime, what we can honestly offer is a structured, human-reviewed intake — and, eventually, tools like a law-enforcement-ready incident report and a pre-filled submission path to NamUs, the Department of Justice's national missing-persons database.

If you are law enforcement and interested in exploring a partnership, we'd like to hear from you.
